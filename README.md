# 🎵 Lyra

---
2026 Feb 26 :
I've just watched this video, which discussed the today's subscription social issues. 
If this keeps going on, people in the future won't own anything. Cars, PCs, e-books, media, printers, and services might all be subscription-based; onwing nothing is terrible.

So I hope in the future, Lyra can offer online service but in **pay as you go**. You pay for the media, which means you buy **the right to use** it.
You also pay for the service—the fee for storing, processing, and transferring your data.
If you download a media to your device, you don't have to pay any derivative expenses except the cost of transferring.

The idea is wonderful, though the real world is not. It's nearly impossible to reach the goal.
While some expenses, such as hosting a portal website, can be brought down to zero using services like GitHub Pages, maintaining a reliable backend still requires funding.
In addition, I do want to charge a slight premium. But instead of lining our pockets, this small profit margin will serve as a forced donation—a way to financially support and give back to the open-source developers who built the foundations we rely on (if they are willing to accept it).

p.s.: If some people prefer subscription, introducing a subscription model as an option is also acceptable🫠. 

But I hope under this kind of payment model, the most important thing is that **the money you pay to be used for your experience, rather than for our advertising and profit**.
